Selected work

Every project is labelled for what it actually is. Client work, prototype and concept are not the same thing and are not presented as if they were.

  • re.born

    Client project. Live.

    A storefront for an upcycled label in Cluj where every piece exists once. That single fact breaks a normal shop, because two people can put the same jacket in a basket and only one of them can have it. Checkout claims the piece with a compare and swap on the row, holds it for 31 minutes, and the Stripe webhook does the rest. It marks the piece sold, releases holds that expired, and refunds automatically if two payments ever land on the same item. The owner photographs a piece and lists it herself, with no developer in the loop, and the Romanian legal pages are already in place.

    The shop opens in October 2026, so payments are still on test keys.

  • Concourse

    Prototype. Invented data.

    A tournament week runs on a group chat. Guests land at different times, flights slip, drivers have to be told, and nobody is holding the current version of the day. Concourse puts all of it on one screen. Arrivals with flight numbers and a delay flag, a driver assigned from a dropdown, a dispatch message ready to send on WhatsApp, a queue of open requests and the day schedule underneath. A coordinator can mark a guest landed, met and delivered without leaving the page.

    Built as a pitch for a sports events company in Cluj-Napoca and modelled on a tournament week. Every guest, flight and organisation in it is invented, and your edits stay in your own browser.

  • UMF Cluj Research Platform

    Live. Approval in progress.

    Finding a research project to join usually means asking around and hoping you hear in time. This platform puts the open projects in one place. Students sign in with their university Microsoft account, apply once, and coordinators work through a scored and ranked review pipeline instead of a mailbox. Row level security in Postgres decides what each account can read, so an applicant sees their own file and nothing else.

    Built with colleagues from EMSA Cluj. Reviewed positively by the Faculty Council, with institutional approval still in progress.

  • MatchSpace

    Collaboration. Live.

    Curated matching events, handled from the signup form all the way to the door. Guests answer a question set and pay through Stripe, then an engine solves the pairings as a maximum cardinality match rather than a first come queue, so the largest possible number of people leave with a match instead of just the first few. Every guest gets a QR ticket and is checked in on a phone camera at the entrance. Behind it sits the back office for events, guests, pairings and takings.

    Built together with Dragos Hapaianu, who owns the upstream repository.

About

I build the boring part properly.

Final year medical student in Cluj-Napoca, building web software full time alongside it. Full stack in practice means I take the whole thing. The database, the money, the emails, the admin screens, and the part where someone has to use it at 7am with one hand.

Medicine is where I learned what a broken process costs. A form filled in wrong, a handover that happened in a corridor, a list that only exists in one person's head. Most of what I build is aimed at exactly that.

I work alone on most projects, from the first sketch to the live domain. When a build is not mine alone I say so and I name the person. I write plain HTML when a site does not need a framework, which is why this page has no build step.

Romanian and English. Based in Cluj-Napoca.
