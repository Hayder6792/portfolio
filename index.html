02  About

Who you would be working with.

I build the whole thing. Design, front end, back end, and the unglamorous parts that keep it running after launch.

I am a medical student in Cluj who kept building things on the side until it became the main thing. That mix turns out to be useful: I have built a research platform for a medical university and a concept site for a psychiatry practice, so I am comfortable with the care that health and clinic work needs.

I work on my own, so you talk to the person writing the code. I would rather tell you early that something is a bad idea than build it quietly and invoice you for it.

When I am not building, I am studying or out running.
